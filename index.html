The Story

A Revolution
Sung Into Being

In 1991, Estonia did the impossible. Without firing a single shot, they sang their way to freedom from Soviet rule.

The Singing Revolution tells the story of Taavi and Sofia, two young lovers caught between duty and desire as their nation rises. When 300,000 Estonians gather to sing forbidden songs, they discover that the most powerful weapon against tyranny is the human voice.

This is not just history. This is a love letter to the power of unity, courage, and the songs that set us free.
